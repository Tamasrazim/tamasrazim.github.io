# Character Reference: Ren Kurogane

## At a glance

- **Age at opening:** 16
- **Home:** A remote mountain village
- **Initial rank:** F-Rank
- **Academy status:** Not eligible at the beginning; must train and qualify
- **Signature colour:** Black
- **Primary discipline:** Swordsmanship, with an unexplained interaction with certain old magic
- **Weapon at opening:** Wooden training sword
- **Narrative job:** Protagonist; determined underdog whose strength grows through work, mistakes, and experience

## Physical design

Ren has messy, layered black hair with uneven bangs, dark charcoal eyes, a lean and practical frame, and a youthful face that is easy to read in close-ups. His hair and clothing should create an identifiable black silhouette without turning him into a generic shadow character. He wears a plain charcoal training kimono, dark trousers, hand wraps, and worn sandals in the village portion of the story.

No expensive armour or legendary sword at the start. His equipment changes when the story gives him a credible reason to replace it.

## Behaviour

Ren is a stubborn idiot in daily life. He takes jokes too far, misunderstands sarcasm, forgets obvious social details, makes excuses after losing, and is spectacularly bad at identifying romantic interest. He can be brave when people are in danger and attentive to a physical opponent's habits, but those capabilities do not make him generally wise.

Keep his comedy characterful rather than making every line a joke. When the situation is serious, his fear and concern should feel sincere. He can make bad decisions and suffer for them.

## Combat development

Ren starts with basic village-taught sword forms and poor experience against trained opponents. His early strengths are persistence, willingness to retry, attention to specific physical cues, and adaptability when a plan fails. His early victories require circumstances, teamwork, and narrow openings; he does not simply overpower stronger enemies.

The shrine incident shows him interrupting a binding line connected to the damaged seal mechanism. The achievement is surprising but limited. The loose sealing pin in his hand causes the sentinel to respond to an old custody protocol. Neither event proves that Ren is a chosen one or secretly divine.

## Family

- **Takeshi Kurogane:** Father, village swordsmith and former soldier. Protective and cautious, but capable of teaching real technique.
- **Aoi Kurogane:** Mother, village healer. Warm but firm; she insists that strength must not erase compassion.
- **Nene Kurogane:** Younger sister, interested in enchanted tools and magical craftsmanship.
- **Isamu Kurogane:** Grandfather whose work repairing weapons during a war left a complicated family reputation.

## Relationships

- **Tori Tsukishiro:** Central love interest and a fierce rival. Ren's obliviousness is a running comedy source, but romance develops through real experiences.
- **Daichi Moriyama:** Dependable squadmate and early friend.
- **Yuna Shirogane:** Sharp-tongued rival and scout; pushes him to improve.
- **Haru Minazuki:** Tactical observer who identifies flaws in Ren's fighting.
- **Sayo Hanabishi, Mio Kazehara, Kanna Yozora, and other characters:** Potential romantic interests whose stories remain independent; attraction must not make their personalities interchangeable.

## Continuity safeguards

- Ren remains foolish as he becomes powerful.
- Do not make him omniscient, cruelly emotionless, or instantly unbeatable.
- Do not allow F-Rank status to be ignored for academy entry.
- Do not make every major historical event revolve around his family.
- Do not explain the shrine through the unrelated unknown god.
