# Character Reference: Tori Tsukishiro

## At a glance

- **Age at opening:** 16
- **Identity:** Royal princess
- **Initial rank:** A-Rank
- **Signature colour:** Crimson red
- **Combat disciplines:** Royal swordsmanship and magic
- **Weapon:** Refined katana with a crimson-lacquered sheath
- **Squad:** Separate elite four-person squad during roughly the first fifty chapters
- **Narrative job:** Second lead, independent protagonist of her own arc, Ren's central love interest

## Physical design

Tori has long crimson hair, ruby eyes, strong expressive eyebrows, and a trained, athletic build. Her silhouette is elegant but practical. Her academy combat clothes combine crimson and black in a traditional Japanese-inspired cut, with restrained gold around the royal crest. A small crimson blossom ornament identifies her royal family. She should look capable of moving, running, drawing a sword, and fighting in this outfit.

At sixteen, her design should read as a believable teen warrior, without sexualised framing. Any later adult-timeskip redesign should retain her distinctive silhouette and become more mature through clothing, posture, and facial details, not a sudden personality replacement.

## Personality

Proud, competitive, blunt, fiercely independent, and hot-tempered. She is accustomed to being among the strongest students and takes challenges personally. She can be generous and protective, but she hates feeling pitied and struggles to admit uncertainty.

Her anger has consequences. She can make a bad situation worse by reacting before thinking. Her growth means learning when to control that impulse, not losing her confidence or becoming permanently gentle.

## Family

- **King Masanori Tsukishiro:** Her father. A disciplined ruler who loves her but measures her choices by their consequences for the kingdom.
- **Queen Akemi Tsukishiro:** Her mother. A former swordswoman whose choice to protect civilians during a military withdrawal remains politically controversial.
- **Prince Seiran Tsukishiro:** Her older brother and heir. Patient and politically skilled, but too inclined to make decisions on Tori's behalf.

The family is not simply cruel. Affection and pressure coexist, and their disagreements should unfold over time.

## Combat design

Tori's swordsmanship is precise and refined. Her crimson magic should be clean and recognizable: controlled lines around the blade, sparks at the point of impact, and bursts used with purpose. Avoid filling every panel with red effects. Her movements contrast with Ren's improvised, adaptive style.

She begins A-Rank, which makes her exceptional among students, but she is not invincible. She can be outmanoeuvred, misread an opponent, or face a problem that cannot be solved by force.

## Relationships

- **Ren Kurogane:** The central romance. Their relationship begins with friction and grows through rivalry, trust, cooperation, embarrassment, and honest conflict. Ren's obliviousness should drive comedy without making every scene identical.
- **Her noble friends:** A small group who can tease her with the private nickname “Flattie” occasionally. The joke is infrequent banter, not the whole friendship. Friends have their own goals and flaws.
- **Male admirers:** Akito Saionji, Haruki Amagiri, Raiga Onizuka, Itsuki Arisawa, and Sora Kazanami have different reasons for pursuing or admiring her. Tori does not owe romantic interest to anyone, and not every admirer must be an antagonist.
- **Her own squad:** Three teammates who challenge her authority, support her, and have stories beyond praising the princess.

## Continuity safeguards

- Tori begins A-Rank, not S-Rank.
- Keep her squad distinct from Ren's squad for about the first fifty chapters.
- She has goals, battles, family conflicts, and decisions that matter when Ren is absent.
- Do not reduce her to jealousy or romance.
- Her anger is loud and comedic at times, but serious scenes must show her judgement, courage, and vulnerability too.
