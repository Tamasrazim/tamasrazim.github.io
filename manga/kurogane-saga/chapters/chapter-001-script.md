# Chapter 1 — The Monster That Knelt Before an F-Rank

**Format:** 28 pages, black-and-white manga, right-to-left reading order  
**Function:** Immediate hook, Ren's everyday personality, the shrine incident, a limited demonstration of his courage, and the first visible trace of the Ashen Banner  
**Important continuity:** Ren is F-Rank and cannot enrol in the academy. The incident does not grant him instant power or admission.

## PAGE 1 — THE FESTIVAL

**Panel 1 — Wide establishing panel.** A mountain village at dusk. Wooden roofs step up the slope. Lanterns hang between buildings, festival stalls line the lane, and a shrine path disappears into cedar trees beyond the last houses. The old shrine itself is visible on the mountain, slightly neglected.

CAPTION: In the village of Kurotake, the festival bell has been silent for twenty-three years.

**Panel 2.** Children run past with paper masks. A vendor turns skewers over a brazier.

SFX: ZZA— ZZA—

**Panel 3.** Ren Kurogane, sixteen, stands in a small clearing with a wooden sword held in both hands. His sleeves are tied back badly.

REN: One clean hit. That's all I need.

**Panel 4.** Across from him, an older village teenager named Genji raises his own wooden sword. He has already heard this speech too many times.

GENJI: You said that yesterday.

REN: Yesterday I needed two clean hits.

**Panel 5 — Small reaction panel.** Two children watch from the edge of the ring. One is holding a snack.

CHILD: Who's winning?

SECOND CHILD: The festival.

## PAGE 2 — THE GREAT SWORD MASTER

**Panel 1.** Genji demonstrates a neat, basic guard. Ren copies the stance, but his rear foot points the wrong way.

GENJI: Your back foot. Turn it out.

REN: It's comfortable.

GENJI: It is also facing the wrong direction.

**Panel 2.** Ren adjusts his foot. His wooden sword is now held backward.

**Panel 3 — Close on Genji's eye twitching.**

GENJI: Why are you holding it like that?

REN: You said to turn it.

**Panel 4.** Genji taps Ren's wrist. Ren's wooden sword flies out of his hands.

SFX: TAK!

**Panel 5.** Ren watches the sword land in the dirt. He looks personally betrayed.

REN: Defective.

GENJI: It's a stick.

REN: A defective stick.

**Panel 6 — Comedy beat.** Aoi, Ren's mother, passes behind the ring carrying a basket of bandages. She doesn't even stop walking.

AOI: Apologise to the stick.

## PAGE 3 — A NORMAL BOY

**Panel 1.** Ren retrieves the wooden sword and brushes off the dirt.

REN: One more round.

GENJI: Go eat something before you embarrass your family again.

REN: My family is used to it.

**Panel 2.** Aoi places a wrapped rice ball in Ren's hand.

AOI: You're allowed to lose. You're not allowed to start a fight over it.

REN: What if I lose unfairly?

AOI: Then lose quietly.

**Panel 3.** Ren bites the rice ball. His little sister Nene, ten, points at his stance.

NENE: Your feet were backwards.

REN: Betrayal. From inside the family.

**Panel 4.** Nene holds up a tiny wooden charm shaped like a bell. She has been carving it.

NENE: I made this. It's not a weapon, so you can't blame it when you lose.

REN: I feel attacked.

**Panel 5 — Gentle family beat.** Aoi laughs despite herself. In the background, Takeshi Kurogane, Ren's father, watches from beside the village forge. He is filing the edge of a work tool and says nothing.

## PAGE 4 — THE STRANGER

**Panel 1 — Long lens composition.** At the far end of the festival, where lantern-light fades, a traveller stands beside the path to the abandoned shrine. Their face is hidden by a plain travel hood.

**Panel 2.** Close on a sleeve shifting in the wind. Beneath it is a narrow strip of ash-grey cloth marked with a simple three-stroke emblem.

**Panel 3.** The traveller looks up toward the shrine bell. Their fingers tighten around a small metal instrument used for lifting old pins.

**Panel 4.** A child runs past, nearly colliding with the stranger.

CHILD: Sorry!

**Panel 5.** The stranger steps aside politely. Their voice is calm.

STRANGER: No harm done.

**Panel 6 — Close on the stranger's eyes.** Their gaze returns to the shrine. No villainous grin. Just concentration.

## PAGE 5 — THIRTEEN

**Panel 1.** The festival drummer lifts his sticks. People gather around a tiny stage.

SFX: DON! DON! DON!

**Panel 2.** At the top of the mountain, the old shrine bell moves by itself.

SFX: GONNNG—

**Panel 3 — Wide.** The first ring rolls across the valley. Conversations stop. Birds burst from the trees.

CAPTION: One.

**Panel 4.** The second ring. A lantern beside the shrine gutters, then turns black.

CAPTION: Two.

**Panel 5.** The bell's clapper is moving without a rope or visible hand.

CAPTION: Three.

**Panel 6.** Back at the festival, Ren has stopped chewing.

REN: …Was the bell supposed to do that?

## PAGE 6 — THE SEAL BREAKS

**Panel 1.** The fourth through seventh rings are represented by overlapping narrow panels, each showing another lantern losing its light.

SFX: GONNG. GONNG. GONNG. GONNG.

**Panel 2.** Beneath the shrine steps, old paper seals peel away from the stone. Their ink runs like wet soot.

**Panel 3.** The stranger starts moving quickly uphill.

**Panel 4.** Takeshi looks toward the mountain. The tool slips from his hand and hits the forge floor.

SFX: CLINK.

**Panel 5.** He grabs a short sword from the wall.

TAKESHI: Aoi. Get the children inside.

AOI: Takeshi, what is it?

TAKESHI: I don't know. That's why.

**Panel 6 — Narrow black panel.** The eighth ring.

CAPTION: Eight.

## PAGE 7 — THE MASK

**Panel 1.** The ninth ring shakes dust loose from the shrine roof.

**Panel 2.** The tenth. A crack opens through one of the old stone guardians flanking the path.

**Panel 3.** The eleventh. People begin running downhill. A festival stall topples.

**Panel 4.** The twelfth. The shrine doors slowly open inward.

SFX: KRRRREEE—

**Panel 5 — Large reveal panel.** The thirteenth ring. In the dark doorway stands a tall, thin figure wearing a cracked white ceremonial mask. Strips of ancient cloth hang from its shoulders, and dim seal-marks run along its arms. It carries no visible weapon.

SFX: GONNNNNG—

CAPTION: Thirteen.

## PAGE 8 — EVERYONE RUNS

**Panel 1.** The masked figure steps off the shrine platform. The lanterns near it go out one after another.

**Panel 2.** A village guard draws his sword.

GUARD: Stop there! Name yourself!

**Panel 3.** The figure turns its mask toward the guard. The guard's sword begins to vibrate violently in his hand.

SFX: VRRRR—

**Panel 4.** The sword snaps at the hilt. The guard falls backward, unhurt but terrified.

**Panel 5.** Takeshi steps in front of Aoi and Nene.

TAKESHI: Downhill. Now.

NENE: What about Ren?

**Panel 6.** Ren is on the other side of the clearing, helping a fallen child stand.

REN: I FOUND HIM! GO!

## PAGE 9 — IT CHOOSES A DIRECTION

**Panel 1.** The sentinel walks past the guards without attacking them.

**Panel 2.** It steps past a crying child. The child goes silent, staring at the mask.

**Panel 3.** Close-up of the mask. Tiny script appears across its surface, like old writing being recalled one stroke at a time.

**Panel 4.** The sentinel turns and begins walking directly toward Ren.

**Panel 5.** Ren notices the creature coming and looks behind himself to see whether someone is standing there.

REN: …Me?

**Panel 6.** He raises his wooden sword. His knees are visibly shaking.

REN: Okay. Just checking. Maybe you're here for someone else.

## PAGE 10 — THE KNEEL

**Panel 1.** Ren backs away from the creature. His heel catches on a broken step.

**Panel 2.** He falls hard. The wooden sword rolls away.

SFX: THUD!

REN: Ow! Fuck!

**Panel 3.** A small black-metal pin slides out of a crack in the shrine stone and skitters across the ground. The stranger, now hidden behind a fallen screen, notices it.

**Panel 4.** The pin stops beside Ren's hand. Its surface carries two interlocking ring marks.

**Panel 5.** Ren snatches it up because the sentinel is reaching toward him and he doesn't know what else to do.

REN: Here! Is this what you want? Take it and stop being creepy!

**Panel 6 — Wide, silent panel.** The sentinel stops. Then it kneels before Ren, one hand pressed to the ground.

No dialogue. No SFX. Let the pause land.

## PAGE 11 — THE WRONG QUESTION

**Panel 1.** Ren stares down at the kneeling sentinel.

REN: …You want me to sign something?

**Panel 2.** The mask tilts upward. A broken voice emerges.

SENTINEL: Keeper…

**Panel 3.** Close on Ren's face. He points at himself.

REN: Wrong guy.

**Panel 4.** The sentinel reaches toward the pin but stops before touching it. Its fingers twitch as if bound by an old rule.

SENTINEL: Where… is the second keeper?

**Panel 5.** Ren looks over his shoulder and shouts toward the fleeing villagers.

REN: ANYBODY HERE A KEEPER? IT'S ASKING FOR ONE!

**Panel 6.** Aoi, pulling Nene downhill, looks back in horror. Takeshi turns sharply toward Ren.

TAKESHI: Ren! Put the pin down!

## PAGE 12 — THE COMMAND FAILS

**Panel 1.** Ren tries to drop the pin. His fingers open, but the old metal catches briefly against the cloth wrap on his palm.

**Panel 2.** The sentinel rises halfway. The script on its mask flickers and tears apart.

SENTINEL: Second keeper absent. Seal incomplete.

**Panel 3.** The shrine stones tremble. A dark, threadlike line stretches from the sentinel's chest to a carved notch beneath the bell platform.

**Panel 4.** Ren sees the line. It is physical-looking magic, taut between two points, pulling the creature back toward the shrine.

REN: Dad! There's a string!

TAKESHI: Don't touch it!

**Panel 5.** The line tightens. A shockwave knocks several loose boards toward the fleeing villagers.

**Panel 6.** Ren looks at the people still trapped near the market stall, then back at the line.

REN: Yeah. Probably should've led with that.

## PAGE 13 — A BAD PLAN

**Panel 1.** Ren runs to retrieve his wooden sword. The sentinel's arm whips toward him.

**Panel 2.** Ren ducks, clumsy and late. The strike tears through the edge of his sleeve and cracks a wooden post behind him.

SFX: KRAK!

**Panel 3.** Ren hits the ground and scrambles backward.

REN: Okay! You're definitely not a festival performer!

**Panel 4.** The sentinel advances. Ren spots the magical line pulsing every time it moves.

**Panel 5.** He remembers Takeshi showing him how to cut rope under tension: never strike where the pull is strongest; find where it is anchored.

**Panel 6.** Ren looks toward the carved notch beneath the bell platform. The notch is partly hidden under a fallen beam.

REN: I hate it when Dad is right.

## PAGE 14 — THE VILLAGERS

**Panel 1.** Takeshi reaches two guards and directs them toward a group pinned behind a fallen stall.

TAKESHI: Get them down the east path! Don't approach the shrine!

**Panel 2.** Aoi wraps a cloth around a villager's bleeding forearm. Nene is holding the basket with both hands, terrified but trying to help.

NENE: Ren's still up there!

AOI: I know.

**Panel 3.** Aoi looks at the mountain path. Her expression is frightened, but she keeps tending to the injured person.

**Panel 4.** Takeshi starts toward Ren, then stops when the sentinel turns. If he advances, it will strike the crowd behind him.

**Panel 5.** He grips his sword and calls out.

TAKESHI: REN! DON'T TRY TO BE A HERO!

**Panel 6.** Ren, crouched behind a broken stone lantern, shouts back.

REN: I WAS TRYING TO BE A COWARD! IT DIDN'T WORK!

## Writer / artist continuity notes

- Ren's only win is a narrow interruption of the sentinel's binding mechanism. He does not defeat it by raw power.
- The sentinel kneels because the black-metal pin temporarily triggers an old custody protocol. Ren is not named by prophecy.
- The mysterious stranger belongs to the Ashen Banner, but the organization does not know what happened fully and does not control every part of the incident.
- The capital archive creates a second thread without explaining too much.
- The academy minimum is E-Rank; Ren remains F-Rank at the end of this chapter.
- Tori does not appear in Chapter 1. Her introduction should be in a later chapter, in her own environment, with enough room to establish her A-Rank status and explosive personality.
- The final page ends with a concrete next goal: train until he qualifies.
