# The Kurogane Saga — Manga Production Workspace

**Status:** Pilot development  
**Working title:** The Kurogane Saga (not the final series title)  
**Format:** Original Japanese-inspired fantasy manga, black-and-white interior pages, right-to-left reading order  
**Production branch:** `kurogane-manga-pilot`

This folder is the working source of truth for the manga. It is deliberately isolated on a branch so that the pilot work does not change or publish the live website.

## What is in this workspace

- `canon/series-bible.md` — core rules, setting, cast structure, antagonist continuity, and long-form story principles.
- `production/art-direction.md` — visual language, character continuity, page readability, and production rules.
- `characters/ren-kurogane.md` — Ren's character reference.
- `characters/tori-tsukishiro.md` — Tori's character reference.
- `chapters/chapter-001-script.md` — the first chapter, with page-by-page panel direction, dialogue, and sound effects.

## Pilot objective

Create a complete, readable first chapter that hooks the reader immediately, establishes Ren's comic personality, introduces the shrine mystery, and plants the Ashen Banner in the story from the opening. Ren is still F-Rank and cannot enter the academy yet. The supernatural incident does not grant him instant mastery or bypass the academy's admission rules.

The first chapter is a story pilot, not a claim that hundreds of future chapters are already complete. The larger cast and long arc will be developed from this stable foundation.

## Canon rules

1. Ren Kurogane and Tori Tsukishiro are both sixteen at the start.
2. Use given-name-first presentation throughout this project: Ren Kurogane, Tori Tsukishiro.
3. Ren begins at F-Rank. F-Rank candidates cannot enrol at the academy; Ren must train and qualify first.
4. Tori begins at A-Rank and belongs to a different four-person squad for roughly the first academy year, about fifty chapters.
5. Magic and swordsmanship are equally important parts of combat.
6. Ren is persistently foolish in everyday life and romantically oblivious. He can learn, fight, and adapt without becoming a cold genius.
7. The Ashen Banner has a continuous storyline from the first chapter. It is not responsible for every bad event in the world.
8. There is only one unknown god in the distant background. It has no established connection to the main plot, Ren, Tori, or the Ashen Banner. No divine hierarchy or god-war plot.
9. The full saga is intended to include roughly 500 distinct characters, each added with a narrative purpose rather than as padding.
10. All artwork must be original in composition and character design. The series can use broad Japanese fantasy manga conventions without copying another series' protected characters, scenes, or plot.

## Current production state

The story bible and first chapter script are drafted. Artwork begins with approved character reference sheets and then proceeds to page layouts, finished panels, lettering, and continuity review. No claim is made that Chapter 1 is already fully illustrated.
