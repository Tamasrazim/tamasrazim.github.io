I’m Robiul Rumman Razim, known online as Tamasrazim. I’m learning and experimenting across computer science, software, visual systems and interactive media.

01 / A little context

Not a finished story.

This website is a record of things I’m trying to understand and make. Some are practical tools. Some are technical experiments. Others begin as visual or interactive ideas and gradually become software.

I prefer showing what exists over describing what might exist. A prototype is a prototype; a research direction is not a finished product. I want the work to make that clear.
