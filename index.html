HIDDEN / ORIGIN01—01

A name can be a place you return to.

TAMASRAZIM

Some names are chosen. Some names are discovered. This one was gathered from pieces of a real story.

“Tamanna’s Tama.”
Tamanna is the person whose name lives inside the first half of it — the source of Tama, the first word carried forward.

And Razim was never borrowed from somewhere else. It came from my own real-life name: Razim.

So one part came from Tamanna, one part came from me, and somewhere between them the pieces stopped feeling separate.

“Tama, carried from Tamanna.
Razim, carried from my real name.
Two fragments, one identity — until the space between them disappeared.”

The final name is Tamasrazim.
Not a replacement for who I am, but a name that remembers where it came from.
