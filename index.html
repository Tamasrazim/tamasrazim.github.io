I work across native Windows software, games, browser tools, and creative projects. I learn by building, testing, and improving things I can actually use.

When existing tools don't fit the job, I experiment with making something that does. I prefer showing what works over making unfinished work sound finished.

ORIGIN RECORD / 001

A name formed from two pieces of a real story.

Tama came from Tamanna — a small piece of a name that stayed with the story.

Razim came from my real-life name, keeping the online identity tied to the person behind it.

The two fragments met. The space disappeared, and the combined name became one identity.

Tamasrazim.

Two fragments, joined into one name.
