HOW TO READ THIS

What this means in practice.

01

Viewing is allowed.

You can visit the public website and use the published browser experience through its normal interface. GitHub's own service terms still govern its hosting and repository features.

02

Reuse requires permission.

Unless an explicit licence says otherwise, don't copy, republish, redistribute, mirror, modify for republication, extract original libraries or datasets, or incorporate original code and assets into another product without written permission.

03

Third-party material is separate.

External fonts, libraries, logos, services and other third-party materials remain subject to their respective owners' licences and terms. A Tamasrazim licence does not change those terms.

04

Exports and user-provided content need context.

This page describes the original software and creative material published by Tamasrazim. It does not automatically determine rights to third-party inputs or every output created by a user of a tool. Check relevant project terms, source material rights and any applicable marketplace or contract terms.

05

Ask before using it elsewhere.

For adaptations, commercial use, redistribution, embedding or other permissions, get written approval for the specific work and intended use. Permission for one project does not automatically cover another.

Need permission for a use?

Send the project or file name, your intended use, where it will appear, and whether the use is commercial. Written permission can specify the scope of the licence.
