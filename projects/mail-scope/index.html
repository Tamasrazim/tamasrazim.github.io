MailScope.

I made MailScope for quick first-pass checks: clean up an email address, look at relevant mail DNS records, and keep notes on what you found.

It checks input and DNS records that are useful for diagnosis. A lookup is only a snapshot, though; it cannot prove a message will reach an inbox or that a domain is secure.
