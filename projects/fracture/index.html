01 / CORE LOOP

Movement is
the puzzle.

FRACTURE is a compact, single-player parkour prototype. Keep momentum through a line of floating platforms, judge the moving ones, recover at checkpoints, and use gravity inversion to change how you approach a gap. Shards reward exploration; the exit beacon ends the sector.

MECHANIC / 01

Momentum

Accelerate, brake, and sprint. Small adjustments to direction matter more than frantic inputs when the next platform is narrow.

MECHANIC / 02

Gravity shift

Invert gravity while airborne to push toward a different surface. Watch the HUD indicator so you know which direction will pull you.

MECHANIC / 03

Moving platforms

Three platform patterns move along different axes. Time the approach, land, and let checkpoints reduce the cost of a failed jump.

02 / INPUT MAP

setup.

On phones and tablets, start the game and use the virtual joystick for movement. Drag on the open play area to look around; the action buttons stay in thumb reach. Landscape orientation gives you more room, but portrait mode remains supported.
