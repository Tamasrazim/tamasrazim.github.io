Privacy · Web PWA

Your reading space.
Your device.

SORYVYN stores imported page images, reading progress, bookmarks, and preferences in this browser. It does not require an account.

What this PWA stores

Saved titles, favorites, reading history, page progress, bookmarks, imported image pages, theme preference, and whether the information notice was dismissed may be stored in browser-managed storage, including the soryvyn.web.chunk01 local-storage key and the SORYVYN IndexedDB library.

This data stays in the browser profile on this device. Clearing site data or switching browser profiles can remove or separate it. JSON backups are downloaded directly by the browser; imported image files are not included in those backups.

Network activity

The source directory links to official publisher and creator platforms. Opening a platform sends requests to that provider and is governed by its own privacy policy. SORYVYN does not scrape or mirror provider chapters. The custom 3D reader displays page images imported by the user.

SORYVYN does not send search terms, favorites, bookmarks, reading progress, or imported page files to a SORYVYN backend. GitHub Pages may process standard request and security information under its own terms and privacy policy.

Offline storage

The service worker caches the application shell so the interface can reopen offline. Imported page images and reading state are stored locally by the browser. Cached application files and local data may remain until the browser removes them or you clear the site's stored data.

Features not currently available

SORYVYN does not currently provide account synchronization or an in-app online chapter-fetching service. Official chapter access remains on provider websites. Any future feature that adds data collection or third-party network activity must be documented here before release.
