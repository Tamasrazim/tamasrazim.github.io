Nori and the Last Light

A storm extinguishes the village lighthouse while a small boat is lost at sea. Nori, a young fox, sets out through the rain, meets a field of frightened fireflies, crosses a broken bridge and discovers an ancient mirror that can return their tiny lights to the shore. The full five-minute story plays with timed subtitles. The artwork is generated in code, frame by frame.

THE STORY

The lighthouse is more than a tower: to the people of the shore, its beacon is a promise that everyone can find their way home. When that promise goes dark, Nori discovers that he does not have to restore the light alone. By gathering many small lights and reflecting them together, he guides the boat back to safety.

ABOUT THIS PREVIEW

This standalone player is for watching and scrubbing the complete animation. To edit the source, open the TRILYVA workspace, choose Examples, then select Nori and the Last Light. In Voice Studio, build the included five-minute WAV and use Finish Movie to combine audio with a rendered film.
