Niko and the Clockwork Bird

Niko, a young red-panda inventor, builds Auri, a tiny brass clockwork bird whose song depends on a missing spring. When the spring rolls into Clocktown’s stopped clock tower, they follow it through the square and into the gears. Auri’s wing is damaged on the way, so Niko repairs it before they work together to restore the clock and bring the first morning bell back to life.

THE STORY

The story follows one clear chain of cause and effect: Auri cannot sing without her heart-spring; the spring rolls into the stopped town clock; the gear mechanism damages Auri’s wing; Niko repairs the wing before they free the spring together. The clock starts, the first bell rings, and Auri finally sings.
