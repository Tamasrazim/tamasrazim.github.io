Pip and the Rainbow Kite

Pip the little fox and Ollie the bluebird build a rainbow kite for Spring Sky Day. A playful gust whisks it across Cloverfield Meadow, and the friends must work together to retrieve it gently, keep it dry through a passing shower, and repair its ribbons before the festival. Seven connected story sequences bring expressive characters, a creek, a flower path, a rainy willow shelter, and a bright rainbow-kite finale.

THE STORY

Pip’s rainbow kite becomes the center of an adventure about patience and teamwork. Each friend brings a different skill: Ollie spots the way, Tuck keeps the crossing steady, and Pip learns that a careful repair can be better than a hurried tug. When the shower passes, they return to the hill and fly the kite together.

ABOUT THIS PREVIEW
