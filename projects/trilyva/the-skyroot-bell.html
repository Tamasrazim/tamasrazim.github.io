The Skyroot Bell

When the golden bell that lights the floating world of Lumenreach disappears, young fox gardener Eira and her lantern-moth Pip follow its echo across singing rivers, whispering woods and drifting islands. The bell's new keeper, a lonely little cloud-dragon, has made a wrong choice for a very understandable reason. Getting the light back will take more than a chase: it will take three small notes, an apology, and a village willing to share.

THE STORY, FROM FIRST LIGHT TO LAST

Eira starts out wanting to prove she can ring the Skyroot Bell on her own. Along the way, she helps a pebble-sprite and faces the whispers in a dark wood. She discovers that Nix has taken the bell to brighten Cloudrest, a place the lantern-lit village has forgotten to notice. Eira does not excuse his choice; she offers a better way to fix the harm. Nix returns the bell, the villagers bring their own lights, and the three notes help the Skyroot grow a bridge to every home.

HOW THIS CARTOON IS BUILT

The characters, scenery, clouds, lanterns, props, highlights, shadows and particles are drawn with Canvas vector paths and gradients. Character designs keep the same proportions and signature details from scene to scene. Every frame is a pure function of its timestamp, so seeking, previewing and exporting do not depend on playback history.

The film is a visual cartoon with timed on-screen dialogue and narration. This build does not include a pre-recorded voice or music track; the scene and cue data live in the JavaScript source.
