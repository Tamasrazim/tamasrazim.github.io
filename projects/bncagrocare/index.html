BUSINESS PROJECT / CATALOGUE + INVOICING

BNC Agro Care.

I built this as two connected pieces: a product catalogue customers can browse, and an invoice workspace for representatives.

The catalogue makes product names and pack sizes easy to find. The invoice side handles customer details, product rows, local drafts, a workbook preview and XLSX export.
